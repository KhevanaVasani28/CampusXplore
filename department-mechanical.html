📝 Description

The Department of Textile Technology was established with the pioneering contribution from The Surat Art Silk Cloth Manufacturers Association (SASCMA) in 1995. Inspite of Textiles being very important to Indian economy, Textile department is available at very few institutes across the country and SCET is proud to have one. Strategically located in textile city Surat, the department is earning reputation as an important centre for quality textile education and textile activities.
