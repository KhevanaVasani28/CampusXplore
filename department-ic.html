📝 Description

Instrumentation & Control (IC) Engineering Department is established in 1997 with sanctioned intake of 30 under Veer Narmad South Gujarat University. Now under Gujarat Technological University, the sanctioned intake is 60. With excellent laboratory and classroom facilities, the IC department has well experienced and skilled teaching and non-teaching staff.
