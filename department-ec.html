📝 Description

The Electrical Engineering Department is led by a strong team of qualified, dedicated, and experienced faculty members including Professors, Associate Professors, and Assistant Professors. It includes well-equipped laboratories such as Electrical Machines Lab, Control Systems Lab, Measurement & Instrumentation Lab, Power Systems Lab, Microprocessor Lab, Power Electronics Lab, and Computer Lab with licensed simulation software.
